# Gemini visual shape lane: private native news reader

## Working directory
Write only under `/Users/helix/.Hermes/workspace/news-reader-app/research/gemini/`.

## Goal
Develop a distinctive visual and interaction direction for a private SwiftUI news reader with Apple News-level craft but its own identity. The app has no ads/sponsored placements/subscription, supports configurable sources and political-leaning labels, renders every article in one consistent house design, and links prominently to the original publisher.

## Confirmed constraints
- Private personal app, iPhone and iPad first.
- Product UI, not a marketing site.
- Native article reading is the heart of the product.
- Political labels: left, center-left, center, center-right, right, N/A. Show confidence and distinguish publisher baseline from article-specific assessment.
- Source attribution must be clear. Original-source link at article top and bottom.
- Handle long-form reporting, breaking news, image-heavy stories, sparse feeds, dark mode, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver.
- Avoid generic card grids, glassmorphism, gradient text, fake dashboards, loud political red/blue coding, and visual imitation of Apple News.
- No em dashes in UI copy.
- No external side effects, purchases, publishing, secrets, or code edits outside this lane.
- This is shape research only. Do not create production SwiftUI code yet.

## Deliverable
Create `/Users/helix/.Hermes/workspace/news-reader-app/research/gemini/REPORT.md` containing:
1. One strong visual concept with a name and physical scene sentence.
2. Color strategy, typography strategy, spacing/rhythm, imagery treatment, and motion principles.
3. Detailed layouts for Today/feed, topic/source view, article reader, source manager, political-methodology sheet, and empty/error states.
4. iPhone and iPad adaptation.
5. How political labels remain informative without dominating or inflaming the interface.
6. Article anatomy from headline through original-source footer.
7. Design tokens in plain language, not code.
8. Three signature details that make the app memorable.
9. Accessibility and anti-slop self-critique.

Be specific enough that another designer can turn this into a high-fidelity prototype. Do not produce a generic list of best practices.
